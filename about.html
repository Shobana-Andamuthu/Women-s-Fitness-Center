About Bloom

A women-only fitness center built on strength, community and joy.

Since 2014

Our story

Bloom began in a small Adyar studio with three trainers and twenty members. Women told us they wanted a place without pressure, and the center grew into a full fitness community with studios, a strength floor, a recovery lounge and more than 5,000 members.
