FAQ

Questions members ask

Everything you need to know before your first class.

Is the center women-only?

Yes. Bloom is a women-only space, from the studios and gym floor to the changing rooms, so every member can train comfortably.

I am a complete beginner. Where do I start?

Book a free trial class. A trainer will assess your goals and fitness level and recommend a starter plan and classes.

What is included in a membership?

Unlimited group classes (Premium and Elite), a personal workout plan, a diet plan, trainer check-ins, lockers and showers.

Can I freeze or change my plan?

Yes. You can pause for up to 30 days per year and upgrade or switch plans any time from your member dashboard.

Do you offer personal training?

Personal training sessions are available with every certified trainer. Elite members include four sessions each month.

What should I bring to my first class?

Comfortable activewear, training shoes, a water bottle and a small towel. Yoga mats and equipment are provided.

Your first class is on us

Book a free trial class and a fitness assessment. No joining pressure, just a warm welcome.
